How it behaves

Marking a day read tears the page off: it lifts from the perforation, slides up and away, and the next day’s page is already underneath. With Reduce Motion it crossfades. Swiping sideways turns to the next or previous day. The torn stubs under the binding are the days already read; the edges under the page are the days still to come.

Printer’s blue belongs to the current day and the one primary action on each screen. Everything else is ink. Read and unread days differ by mark length, never by a second colour. No streaks, no scores; a gap is just the next page.

At xxxLarge the day number shrinks a step and the page scrolls; the verse moves below the fold rather than squeezing the reading.

Native notes

iPhone: tab bar for the four sections, the assistant and the paywall as sheets with swipe to dismiss, SF Pro for every control, SF Symbols for icons, Dynamic Type. Android: Material navigation bar, modal bottom sheets, system and predictive Back, Roboto for controls, Material Symbols, font scale, 48 dp targets. Both keep the same pages, numeral and serif.

Every control has at least a 44 pt (iOS) or 48 dp (Android) hit area, including the underlined text links. Ads appear only for free readers, as the one banner at the foot of Plan and other secondary screens, never on Today, the assistant or the paywall.

Paywall copy follows decisions already made: the subscription covers running costs, up to 30 questions a day, monthly and yearly, a yearly price review that passes savings on. Prices are not set.
